Ducky Piano privacy policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

This policy covers Ducky Piano, the Android game published on Google Play under the package name com.kaindl.ducky by Julian Kaindl. In short, the game does not collect, store or share any personal data. Nothing it hears or records ever leaves your phone.

Audio from other apps

Ducky Piano builds its tiles from the music you play in another app, like your music player. To hear that music, it uses Android's audio playback capture feature. Android asks for two things before this works.

  • The microphone permission. Android requires this permission for any audio capture, including capturing the sound of other apps. Ducky Piano never turns on or records from the microphone.
  • Screen sharing consent. Android uses its screen sharing prompt to let an app capture other apps' audio. Ducky Piano only uses the sound. It never captures, records or looks at what is on your screen.

The game analyzes that audio in memory, while you play, to find the beat and tempo. It never saves the audio to a file and never sends it anywhere. When you stop playing, or stop the capture from Android's status bar, the game stops listening.

Android shows a notification and a status bar indicator for as long as the game is listening.

Data stored on your phone

The game saves your best scores, which are each combo you reached and the date and time you reached it. This list stays in the game's own storage on your phone. If you use Android's built-in backup, Android may include the list in your device backup with Google, the same as for other apps. Uninstalling the game deletes the list.

What the game doesn't do

  • It has no internet access, because it doesn't request Android's internet permission.
  • It has no accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics, crash reporting or in-app purchases.
  • It doesn't share data with anyone, because it doesn't collect any.

Google Play

Google runs Google Play, which handles downloads, updates and reviews. Google may collect data about that under its own privacy policy. Ducky Piano doesn't receive that data.

Children

Ducky Piano is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date at the top. If a future version of the game starts to collect data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
